\noindent\textbf{Declaration.}
This team used artificial intelligence (AI) tools in this competition for auxiliary work: information retrieval, code implementation, figure plotting, language polishing and typesetting. The choice of modelling assumptions, the selection of algorithms, the interpretation of results and all conclusions were made by the team members. Every item produced with AI assistance -- code, formulas, data and text -- was checked, recomputed and rewritten by the team; anything found to be wrong, ill-posed or unverifiable was corrected or deleted (see C.3). The tool is listed as item [13]; the full record is in the supporting material.

\noindent\textbf{C.3\quad Verification and corrections (Art. 4)}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
  \item \textbf{E1} Non-connected edges of the shortest-path matrix were initialised to $0$, so Dijkstra reported 9.06 m for a route of about 53 m $\rightarrow$ set to $\infty$.
  \item \textbf{E2} Free space was tested against the 1.5 m-inflated rectangles, which silently demanded a 3 m clearance and misclassified 935 of 20\,000 points $\rightarrow$ recomputed against the raw building outlines.
  \item \textbf{E3} The 8-neighbour grid path overestimated length by about 8\% $\rightarrow$ replaced by a backtracked polyline with string pulling, plus a 0.005 m sampling audit for wall crossing.
  \item \textbf{E4--E6} The feasible arc fragmented under floating-point jitter; in Q2(1) the tie-break rule was overridden by the metaheuristic, and local refinement produced an ill-conditioned $\gamma=2^\circ$ station $\rightarrow$ added a $10^{-6}$ tolerance with circular scanning, a $10^{-4}$ tie-break threshold and a $\gamma\ge15^\circ$ guard.
  \item \textbf{E7--E9} A double degree-to-radian conversion gave an intersection angle of $-812^\circ$; in Q4 a cache keyed on object identity returned a stale matrix, finished alerts re-entered the simulation, single-vehicle alerts counted as served, and the aggregate compared globally rather than per scenario $\rightarrow$ all corrected; only then did the 93 scenarios separate.
  \item \textbf{E10--E12} Three reference DOIs resolved to unrelated papers and two records were incomplete $\rightarrow$ re-queried and corrected or deleted; missing \TeX{} packages and a citation-style mismatch $\rightarrow$ packages replaced and \texttt{natbib} loaded.
\end{itemize}
